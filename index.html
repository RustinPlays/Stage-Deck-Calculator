How to use the stage planner

Mouse controls

  • Click a deck to select it; drag it to move it. Drag on empty canvas to box-select.
  • Ctrl/Cmd-click adds or removes a deck from the selection. Selected decks move and edit together.
  • Click an Add deck size to place automatically, or drag it onto the grid to choose its position.
  • Hover just outside a deck corner for the rotation cursor. Drag around the selection center to rotate in 90° steps; reverse the drag to turn back. Click a corner arrow for one right turn.
  • Right-click empty grid space for Add deck options. Right-click a deck for edit actions.
  • Right-drag to pan the grid; scroll to zoom around the pointer.

Keyboard shortcuts

Select all decks
